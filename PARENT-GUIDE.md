# 👨‍👩‍👧 Lamora — Parent & Caregiver Guide

Welcome! Lamora is designed so a 5-year-old can use it without constant help, while **you** stay in control of time, content and data.

## Getting started

1. Open Lamora (or install it — see the README). Two demo profiles, *Maya (6)* and *Leo (9)*, are included so children can start straight away.
2. Tap a profile to start. Everything adapts to the profile's age (5–10 → three difficulty levels).
3. To make it yours, open the **Parent Zone** and rename the profiles, set ages and pick avatars.

## Opening the Parent Zone

- **Press and hold the 🔒 button in the top bar for 3 seconds**, or use the *Parent zone* button on the profile screen.
- The first time, you'll create a **4-digit PIN** (entered twice). After that the PIN is required every time.
- The screen-time clock pauses while you're in the Parent Zone.

> Forgot the PIN? Clearing the browser's site data resets the app (this also deletes progress).

## What you can control

### ⏳ Screen time
- **Daily limit** per day: 10, 15, 20, 30, 45, 60, 90 or 120 minutes.
- Children get a gentle **5-minute** and **1-minute** warning, then a calm break screen with a physical activity suggestion ("stretch like a cat", "drink some water"…).
- Extending time requires the parent PIN (*Reset today's timer*).
- **Reward game sessions** are separately limited (3–10 minutes each).

### 🏆 Rewards (the learning-to-play loop)
1. Your child completes a learning activity and earns stars.
2. Every *N* stars (you choose 3–10) unlocks **one** short play session.
3. The play session ends automatically after the time you set.

You can pick the reward style (⭐ stars, 💎 gems, 🐚 shells, 🥚 dragon eggs, 🌈 rainbow points, 🪐 planet badges) and reset rewards at any time. There are **no purchases, no loot boxes, no manipulative mechanics** — stickers unlock on a simple, visible ladder.

### 📚 Content
Enable or disable any category: Numeracy, Literacy, Memory Gym, Reward games, Drawing, Colouring, Explore, Chess, Stories, Profession cards, Sticker book. Disabled categories disappear from the child's home screen.

### 🧒 Profiles
Up to 6 local profiles, each with name/nickname, age (5–10), avatar and its own progress, stickers and screen-time count. Progress is never compared between siblings and there are no leaderboards.

### 🔊 Sound & accessibility
Mute all sounds and speech, set volume, and turn on **large text**, **high contrast** or **reduced motion**. Spoken instructions use your device's built-in voices, so they work offline.

### 🛡️ Data
- **Export progress summary** — downloads a small text file you can keep or share with a teacher.
- **Change PIN**.
- **Delete all data** — wipes every profile, drawing, saved photo and setting from the device (double-confirmed).
- Individual profiles can be deleted, and drawings can be deleted from the gallery.

## About photos ("What Can I Be?" cards)

A child (with your help) can add a photo to a profession dream card. The photo is read directly into the page on the device, drawn onto the card preview and **never uploaded anywhere** — Lamora makes no network requests with it, performs no face recognition and no analysis of any kind. The photo is kept only in memory while the card is open; it is not saved unless you export the finished card yourself, and there is a *Remove photo* button.

## Tips for great sessions

- Sit together for the first session and let your child show *you* how it works.
- Short daily practice beats long weekly marathons — the default 30-minute limit is a good start.
- The break screen suggestions are lovely little rituals — join in!
- Chess: start with *The Board* and the piece lessons before *Play the Computer*.

## Troubleshooting

- **No sound?** Check the 🔊 button in the top bar, the volume setting in the Parent Zone, and your device's silent switch.
- **No speech?** Speech uses your device's voices; on some systems the first tap enables audio. Everything works without speech too.
- **App won't update?** When a new version is available online, a banner appears — tap *Update*.
- **Storage full?** Delete old drawings in the gallery, or old profiles in the Parent Zone.
